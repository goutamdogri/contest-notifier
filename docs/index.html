Contest Notifier

A personal, open-source tool that watches programming contest schedules (Codeforces, AtCoder, CodeChef, LeetCode) and syncs upcoming contests to the owner's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

It is not a hosted service and has no other users. It is authorised once by the owner and runs locally on the owner's machine.

Source code: github.com/goutamdogri/contest_notifier
