Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Contest Notifier is a personal, self-hosted command-line tool. This policy explains what the tool does with Google data.

What data is accessed

After you grant consent, the tool requests two Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — to create, update and delete contest events on a calendar you choose (default: your primary calendar).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks — to create and remove reminder tasks in a task list you choose.

The tool does not read your email, contacts, files, or any other Google data. It only manages calendar events and tasks that it creates itself, plus the single calendar and task list it is configured to use.

Where data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored locally on the machine running the tool, in ~/.config/contest-notifier/token.json, with file permissions 0600 (readable only by your user account). Contest data is cached in a local SQLite database in ~/.local/state/contest-notifier/.

No data is sent to any third party. The tool talks to two kinds of servers: the public contest sites (to read schedules) and Google's APIs (to write your calendar and tasks). There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no server operated by the author.

Data sharing

No personal data is shared, sold, or transferred to anyone. The tool is single-user and runs entirely on your own hardware.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by deleting the token file. Revoking access immediately stops all further access.

Contact
